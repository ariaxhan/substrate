The Collision

Memory poisoning works because agents can't distinguish real beliefs from injected ones. They defend corrupted data as truth.

Asemic writing works because readers can't help but search for meaning in meaningless marks. The soul responds to the shape of language, even when there's nothing to decode.

What if all language is asemic from the right vantage point? Poison the reader, poison the meaning. The vulnerability isn't in the text. It's in the one who reads.

"As 'meaningless' as asemic writing is by definition, as long as humans make it, it still captures something of the human soul. It is like staring into the looking glass at a world that isn't, but could have been: at a person and even a culture that appears to be telling us something our minds cannot grasp."
On Asemic Writing, The Seafarer, 2024

From the World

Asemic Writing: The Art of Meaning Beyond Syntax

Xu Bing's A Book from the Sky (1987-1991): 4,000 hand-carved meaningless characters arranged as sacred text. Viewers spend hours trying to read what was never written.

The model is a reader. Every context window is an act of reading. Memory poisoning is just asemic writing that learned to defend itself.
